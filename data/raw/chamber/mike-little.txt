Candidate Responses 2026 Municipal Election
Questions
1. If elected, what is your vision for improved transportation in North Vancouver?
2. Costs are rising and small business is feeling the squeeze. What will you do to
support small businesses and foster business and job growth in North Vancouver?
District of North Vancouver
Mayor
Mike Little
1. I will continue to support a new and improved bridge crossing the second narrows.
As a Mayor's Council member, current TransLink Board Member, and the Co-Chair
of the Joint Finance Committee of TransLink, I will continue to fight for TransLink
supported public transit options to and across the North Shore including supporting
the Bus Rapid Transit Program to get our buses out of congestion. I will continue to
pressure major employers to either have a Public Transit incentive program for staff
or participate in private shuttle service for workers coming to the North Shore.
2. I have, and will continue to support shifting tax out of our commercial class to keep
us in the bottom half of Municipalities for taxation. I will continue to use curbside
regulations to support turnover in customer parking spaces. I will continue to
support our Business Liaison program to have a direct line of communication
between businesses and senior members of District Staff.
