Candidate Responses 2026 Municipal Election
Questions
1. If elected, what is your vision for improved transportation in North Vancouver?
2. Costs are rising and small business is feeling the squeeze. What will you do to
support small businesses and foster business and job growth in North Vancouver?
District of North Vancouver
Councillor
Stephanie Wilson
1. I believe that we can’t just pave our way out of traffic congestion. My priority is
public transit: building out a fast and reliable transit network to make it a real
alternative to driving. Each person taking transit is one less car on the road
contributing to congestion. Active mobility infrastructure is an important tool to
keep people safe and provide another alternative to driving. It also helps people get
to transit stops. Transit planning starts at the rider’s front door, not their bus stop.
There are so many stops that don’t have sidewalk access, benches, or bus shelters.
We need these improvements to make transit more accessible to more people. I do
want to see a Skytrain to the North Shore, but realistically this will take many years.
In the short- and medium-term, we need Rapidbus and Bus Rapid Transit. RapidBus
service to Lynn Valley is in Translink’s plan. Two areas that I feel also need
investment are east of the Ironworkers and on Capilano Road. I understand that it’s
hard to buy into a plan when so many neighbourhoods have poor transit access. The
R2 shows us what’s possible when we make good transit a priority.
2. 1. Find creative ways to use empty storefronts, such as pop-up activations that
showcase businesses and bring energy to streets/shopping areas without the
burden of a long-term lease.
2. Build housing that workers can afford so that good workers stay on long-term
rather than leave a job due to a soul-crushing commute. Help businesses retain
workers by making North Vancouver a good place for workers to live.
3. Encourage reasonable home-based businesses and ground-floor commercial in
new housing developments to help businesses bring their products and services
where people already live.
4. Advocate for fairer commercial leases.
