Candidate Responses 2026 Municipal Election
Questions
1. If elected, what is your vision for improved transportation in North Vancouver?
2. Costs are rising and small business is feeling the squeeze. What will you do to
support small businesses and foster business and job growth in North Vancouver?
City of North Vancouver
Councillor
Ken Izatt
As a retired business owner, I understand what it takes to run a viable business.
Our transportation network needs immediate attention, starting with a collaborative review
of our City’s 2022 Mobility Strategy to ensure our infrastructure keeps people and goods
moving efficiently.
At the same time, supporting small business must be a top municipal priority as it is the
backbone of our local economy. Fostering job growth in North Vancouver requires targeted
policies that reduce friction and lower overhead for local operators:
• Streamline Permitting & Licensing: Fast-track commercial permits and implement a
single-window digital portal to cut wait times for business openings and
renovations.
• Protect Commercial Space: Safeguard ground-floor retail and light industrial zones
along key corridors like Lonsdale and Marine Drive against conversion into purely
residential developments.
• Cut Red Tape: Permanently simplify processes for patio, outdoor retail, and flexible
space operations.
• Support Local Innovation: Partner with local business associations and institutions
to drive shop-local initiatives and worker retention.
Building a resilient, local economy requires smart transportation and real support for
business owners.
