Candidate Responses 2026 Municipal Election
Questions
1. If elected, what is your vision for improved transportation in North Vancouver?
2. Costs are rising and small business is feeling the squeeze. What will you do to
support small businesses and foster business and job growth in North Vancouver?
District of North Vancouver
Councillor
Lisa Muri
1. We need a new bridge, IWM, expansion of the Port requires it, we can address some
congestion with reallocating lanes and improving efficiency. We are beyond the
point of improvement without big capital moves.
2. Small business needs DNV support, faster permits, less bureaucracy, zoning to help
attract, streamlined policy’s, reduced service fees, review of policies creating
barriers negatively impacting small business.
