Biography

submitted to City of North Vancouver's elections office by the candidate

Sean Alexander Candidate for City Council, City of North Vancouver

Independent Representation, Not Agendas

Every four years, we vote for a new City Council. A Councillor’s job isn’t to play politics, serve interest groups, or follow slates and lobbyists, it is to represent you.

When looking at slates and interest groups on municipal committees, ask yourself: Are they truly representing you, or pushing a pre-set agenda that ignores your needs and wants?

You shouldn’t wake up to sudden developments in front of your house without warning. Decisions at City Hall must be made transparently and accountably. Government must work for residents of North Vancouver, not agendas.

Proactive Communication & Year-Round Commitment

My mandate is simple: bring people together rather than campaign on hot-button topics. Proactive transparency on City plans, projects, and bylaws isn't an extra service, it is an elected official's core job.

As your City Councillor, my commitment lasts all four years:

• No Surprises: Clear, upfront communication on local projects before shovels hit the ground.

• Direct Access: Open channels for residents to reach Council without red tape.

• Year-Round Service: Continuous door-knocking, returning phone calls, and remaining accessible.

On October 17th, vote Sean Alexander for City Council.
