Krishna Raisinghani

Independent for City of North Vancouver Council
AboutPromisesContact
My policies are driven by a simple test: will this make everyday life better as North Vancouver grows? I look at who benefits, who carries the impact, whether infrastructure can support it, what it will cost, and how results will be measured and shared publicly.

Read more

If elected, my goal would be a city where more people can find a home they can afford, move through their neighbourhood safely, build a local business, and understand how decisions affecting them are made.

I want growth measured by more than the number of units approved. It should deliver the right homes, infrastructure and services that keep pace, welcoming public spaces, climate resilience and clear community benefits.

Every major decision should also reflect sound financial judgment. That means testing assumptions, considering long-term costs and trade-offs, listening to the people affected, and making the numbers understandable.

And the City should report back. Residents deserve to know what’s on track, what’s delayed, what was spent and whether a policy produced the result it promised.

My goal is practical progress people can see and feel in their daily lives, shaped with the community and delivered with care.

Biography

submitted by the candidate or their team
My family’s North Vancouver story began more than 30 years ago, when my parents ran a small convenience store near 15th and Lonsdale. They chose this community as the place to build their life, and it became the place that shaped mine.

I grew up on the North Shore. The neighbourhoods, local businesses, waterfront and trails aren’t just places on a map to me. They’re part of what makes North Vancouver feel like home.

I also learned early that decisions made at City Hall eventually land on someone’s kitchen table.

I carried that lesson into a career in finance and strategy, helping organizations navigate complex decisions, exercise fiscal responsibility and turn ideas into action.

Today, I serve as Chair of the City’s Advisory Planning Commission, where I’ve spent the past several years listening, learning and providing thoughtful feedback about how our city grows.

I bring deep North Shore roots, direct planning experience and nearly two decades of experience in strategy, finance and organizational leadership.

Reason for running

submitted by the candidate or their team
I’m running for Council because I believe growth should make everyday life better. This community gave my family a place to build a life, and I’m ready to put my experience to work for the place I call home.

That means homes people can afford, with the infrastructure and services to support them.

It means neighbourhoods where a child can safely walk or bike to school, where seniors can move around comfortably, and where our public spaces feel welcoming to everyone.

And it means a City Hall that brings people in earlier, explains its decisions clearly, follows through on its commitments, and treats public money and public trust with care.

North Vancouver helped shape who I am and the values I carry with me. Now, with the community, I want to help shape its next chapter.
