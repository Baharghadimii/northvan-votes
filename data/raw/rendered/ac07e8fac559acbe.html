Are You Eligible to Vote?

To vote as a resident elector in the City of North Vancouver, you must:

be a Canadian citizen;
be 18 years of age or older on the day you register or on General Voting Day;
live in the City of North Vancouver, including Ustlawn – Mission 1 Reserve;
have lived in British Columbia for at least six months immediately before registering; and
not be disqualified from voting.

Permanent residents who have not yet become Canadian citizens cannot vote in municipal elections.

Voter Registration

Register to Vote

The City of North Vancouver uses the provincial voters list.

If you have voted in a previous provincial or local election and your name and address have not changed, you may already be registered.

To register, confirm your registration, or update your name or address in advance, visit Elections BC.

Advance registration deadline: August 18, 2026.

Register or Check Your Voter Registration

Missed the advance registration deadline? You can still register when you vote during advance voting, on General Voting Day, or when requesting a mail ballot.

Identification

What ID Do I Need?

To register at a voting place, bring two pieces of identification that prove your identity and residential address. At least one piece must include your signature.

Examples may include:

BC Driver's Licence
BC Services Card
Canadian passport
Utility bill
Property tax notice
Credit or debit card
Library card

If neither document shows your residential address, you may be able to make a solemn declaration.

Other Voting Options

Mail Ballot Voting

All eligible electors may apply to vote by mail. Further application details will be published by the City of North Vancouver.

Accessible Curbside Voting

Accessible curbside voting will be available for voters who are unable to enter the voting place.
