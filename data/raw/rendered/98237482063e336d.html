Get involved

There's a way to help that fits your Saturday.

In 2022, only 22.64% of us voted. I'm running to change what City Hall does with your trust, but I can't change that number alone. Whether you've got a lawn, an hour, or a free Saturday, there's a way to help that fits.

Why I'm asking for your help

Many of you want to see real change at City Hall. I'm running because I share that goal, and because real change takes more than talk, it takes people willing to organize, volunteer, and show up. If that's you, I'd welcome your help. Volunteer, donate, or send me your questions through Ask Mike.

Voting by mail

All eligible electors can apply to vote by mail ballot.

  1. Complete the Mail Ballot Voting Application Form online from August 31 to October 1, 2026, at 11:59 p.m.: www.vrp.voterview.ca/index/8010/
  2. Mail ballot packages with voting instructions will be mailed to eligible electors in late September/early October.
  3. Complete the ballot and required documents and return the sealed mail ballot package (via mail, in person, or by courier) to the Chief Election Officer before 8:00 p.m. on October 17, 2026. It's on you to make sure your ballot is returned before the deadline.
Special voting for patients of healthcare facilities

Special voting opportunities will be available and provided to patients of the following healthcare facilities:

  • Lions Gate Hospital & Hope Centre - October 10, 2026, 9:00 a.m. - 4:00 p.m.
  • Evergreen House & Northshore Hospice - October 13, 2026, 9:30 a.m. - 2:30 p.m.
