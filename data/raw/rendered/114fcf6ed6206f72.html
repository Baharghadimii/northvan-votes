Committees and Volunteer Work in the Community

Family Services of the North Shore

Community ambassador

I am happy to highlight my role as a community ambassador for Family Services. I have been a valued supporter who has helped amplify their amazing work and helped to strengthen connections across the North Shore community.

  • Christmas Bureau – promote and support via PACs and community – it all started with “The Giving Tree” at my kids school and has grown incredibly across the north shore
  • Bike Donations every Christmas – promote and support this initiative – Obsession Bikes – “Wonder Wheels” – so many community members donate their bikes now
  • Embody – formerly called PEDAW – Provincial Eating Disorders Awareness Week – February 1 to 7 every year
  • Raised awareness on the north shore to wear purple and support those dealing with eating disorders – shared out resources, information to get support and got organizations to light up purple to show their support for the campaign
  • Volunteer at the Community HUB during different times of the year
  • Grad Shop – collaborated with NVSD – SD44 Dresses Campaign – now run by Family Services
  • Recognized each year by them for “Volunteer Appreciation Week”
  • Attend their AGM Meetings, Fundraisers, Community Events and Gala Celebrations

I have been a champion for Family Services in helping to raise awareness of their other programs, connecting people to all their resources, and encouraging others in the community to get involved and give back.

I am honored to be a part of their family and look forward to many more years collaborating together.

MONOVA – Museum and Archives of North Vancouver

School Programs & Sensory Friendly Programs Working Groups
  • Promote and share their educational programs and resources to parents and teachers
  • Participated with students and teachers – on site gallery programs
  • Collaborated with staff to include a sensory room at the new museum – inclusive lens
  • Promote and support their initiative – Sensory Friendly Sundays – every 3rd Sunday/month
  • Promote and share out their events, programs, resources and information
  • Collaborate with staff and ED – Zoe each school year
  • Promote the Vancouver’s North Shore Tourism Association “Neurodiverse Sensory Backpack Program” – available to pickup at MONOVA
  • Organized visit for Japanese Educators to see the sensory spaces and showcased indigenous learning and programs at MONOVA

North Shore Women’s Centre

Committee Member and Volunteer Role with many initiatives
  • Flip the Script Program – Advisory Committee Member
  • Safe Dates Program – Committee Member
  • Neurodivergent Empowerment Program – Advisory Committee
  • Advocated and collaborated with staff to MP Jonathan Wilkinson for grant funding – got approved for a federal grant for the Flip the Script Program and Safe Dates
  • Made connections with NVSD and WVSD high schools and PACs
  • Community outreach related to program support, resource and event sharing
  • Attend their AGM Meetings, Fundraisers, Events and Gala Celebrations

Other volunteer work in the North Shore community

Community Living BC (CLBC)

Accessibility Committee Member
  • Served on CLBC’s Accessibility Committee for 2 years, providing advice and guidance to support implementation of the Accessible BC Act and improve accessibility across the organization.
  • Brought a lived-experience perspective as the parent of a youth transitioning to adult services, helping identify barriers families face when navigating government systems, supports, and services.
  • Advised on accessibility, inclusion, communication, and service improvements to reduce barriers for people with disabilities and their families.
  • Contributed to the development and review of CLBC’s Accessibility Plan and Strategic Plan.
  • Participated in a user-driven project to develop accessible infographics and visual resources that help people better understand how CLBC services, supports, and processes work.
  • Supported a provincial Crown corporation in creating more accessible, inclusive, and person-centred services through a family and lived-experience lens.

BC Children’s Kelty Mental Health Resource Centre

Parent & Caregiver Advisory Council member for 4 years
  • Panel Speaker – Language Matters! Introducing a New Resource for Families on Mental Health and Substance Use
  • Provided feedback on Family Guide for Parents and website resources developed for parents
  • Share out Kelty Centre resources to North Vancouver School District North Shore Standing Committee on Substance Use
  • Share out and showcase Kelty Centre mental health, substance use and well-being resources to parents and schools

Cops for Cancer – Tour de Coast

Promoted and supported the riders – for over 10 years
  • Attend the NV and WV stops during their ride – at schools, both NV RCMP and WVPD Detachments, Park Royal Mall and community business locations
  • Encouraged NV Schools to do fundraising and organized school stops
  • Send out their fundraising links for all north shore riders

North Shore Restorative Justice Society

Member of Restorative Schools Advisory Committee
  • Support their circles in schools programs – have attended and taken part in as well
  • Attend their symposiums, gala fundraisers, AGM Meetings, community dialogues

Polygon Gallery

  • Promote their educational programs for students at the gallery – class field trips for school aged children
  • Promote “Kids First Weekends” – an opportunity the first weekend of each month for families of all kinds to create an in-house art project, based on the exhibitions being shown in the Gallery, for children ages 5 to 12 years old
  • Attend annual fundraiser events in support of The Polygon’s exhibition and educational programming – all of which is offered by donation

Harvest Project

  • Promote their programs, services and resources for the community
  • Share out their community events and fundraisers – thrift store, emergency food program, rent bank and donations for food and clothing
  • Collaborated with them over the years on the North Shore Food Network and North Shore Table Matters
