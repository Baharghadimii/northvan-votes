North Vancouver School District

I believe that a high-quality public education system is essential for the future
of our children, and our world.


Every day, students learn within ecosystems shaped by people, buildings, and policies; and their wellbeing
depends on how well we steward these systems.


I am committed to responsible, transparent oversight of district resources, ensuring that financial and
physical investments directly support student learning.


I believe that school districts should be leaders in our communities: open, inclusive, and welcoming to all
learners and their families.


Most importantly, I want our district to champion the voices of students. While not yet old enough to vote,
their experiences, hopes, and challenges deserve to shape the decisions we make.


I’m running to help steward a school district where every student can thrive, every family feels included, and
every decision reflects our shared commitment to a strong public education system.

Why Vote FOr Me?

I understand how school districts work.

With five years of experience as as school district employee, I’ve seen
first-hand how governance, policy, and operational decisions shape daily
learning.

I am connected to this community as a parent to two elementary aged children who attend school in a NVSD school.

I am focused on aligning decisions with student learning, ensuring budgets, policies, and long-term planning directly support student wellbeing, sustainability, and healthy school communities.

I believe in long-term thinking, recognising that school board decisions have impacts far beyond a four-year election cycle.

I value curiosity and critical thinking, approaching issues by listening, asking questions, and seeking a deep understanding before making decisions.

I care about community and stewardship, shaped by my career in outdoor, environmental, and sustainability education.

I care about the future of young people (mine and yours). Listening to
and acting on the perspectives of students creates a school system that
empowers future citizens.

I’m a long-time resident of North Vancouver, a parent to two elementary-aged children,
and a lover of beautiful, wild places.


I have a MA in Environmental Education and Communication. My professional life has
been rooted in community education, specifically outdoor, environmental, and
sustainability learning. I have spent my career helping people to connect with nature,
with each other, and with the places they call home.


I am an active volunteer. As a youth baseball coach with the North Shore Baseball
Association where I get to support kids as they grow, learn, and build confidence
through sport. And on my school’s PAC, where I focus on fun ways to encourage safe and
active travel to and from school.


I feel deeply connected to the North Shore. I’m a nature lover. You can find me and my
family skiing in the winter, paddling in the summer, and on our bikes whenever we can.
These experiences shape my values and my belief in the importance of community,
stewardship, and creating opportunities for young people to thrive.


I believe that curiosity strengthens community: when we listen to one another, ask
questions, and stay open to learning we make better decisions together. This mindset
shapes how I work, how I lead, and how I show up in my community.
