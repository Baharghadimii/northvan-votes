Meet Mike

A record you can check, not just a promise.

My vision for this city starts with putting residents and business owners back at the centre of our focus.

North Vancouver has been part of my life for as long as I can remember, and my roots here go back decades. I live in the City of North Vancouver today, the city I'm running to lead. We raised our three children here. I built a software company here that created more than 20 highly skilled, well-paying local jobs. After a long career in business, I'm stepping away from the private sector to return to public service - because this community has given me a lot, and I'm not done giving back.

Why I'm running
Canada Day at Civic Plaza
Community

Canada Day at Civic Plaza

I'm one of six directors of the not-for-profit that brought Canada Day to Civic Plaza this year. It was led by volunteers, staffed by volunteers and supported by City staff, and it was a community initiative from start to finish. I put my own time into it because building community and bringing neighbours together is one of the best things we can do for this city.
