A Fourth-Generation North Vancouverite

Betty Forbes is proud of her heritage and has deep roots in the community she serves. She raised her two sons in Lynn Valley, served on their school PACs and board committees, and has been a lifelong volunteer. As an accountant with over 30 years of experience, Betty brings financial discipline and a collaborative approach to Council — listening to community voices and delivering common-sense solutions.
