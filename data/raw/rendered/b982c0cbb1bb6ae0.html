    About Me

     I have been proud to serve as a City of North Vancouver Councillor since 2018, and as a board member of board of the North Vancouver City Library, North Van Arts Council, and Lonsdale Energy, chair of the climate and environment task force, member of the Metro Vancouver climate action and Indigenous relations committees, and as the council representative on the City’s integrated transportation committee.

    I believe strongly in collaboration and partnerships, and currently sit as 3rd Vice-President on the Executive of the Lower Mainland Local Government Association, on the Board of Directors of Climate Caucus, and a member of the BC Municipal Climate Leadership Council, where I'm proud to work with other local and provincial elected officials to find solutions to our challenges.

    For over twenty years I've worked in leadership positions advocating for change on sustainability issues, working to move the public and private sectors as well as individuals on clean energy and climate. This has included positions as Executive Director and Board Director of clean energy and community non-profits and volunteering with local organizations.

     

    I have lived in the City of North Vancouver since 2006, raising my girls, contributing to a thriving community, and spending as much time on the trails and ski runs of the North Shore.
