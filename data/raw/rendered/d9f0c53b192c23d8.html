The bigger picture

The next generation’s future is tied to every city priority.

Next North Van isn’t a separate promise. It’s a way to test whether decisions across City Hall make it easier for every generation to live here, participate, and plan ahead.

01

Open doors, every voice

Make it easier for people who don’t usually follow local government to take part before decisions are settled.

02

Clear, open decisions

Explain what’s changing, why it matters, and how residents can weigh in without needing to be political experts.

03

Fair today and tomorrow

Consider how major costs and commitments affect residents now, and the people who will inherit them.

04

Growth for every generation

Connect housing and growth to transportation, public services, and the everyday cost of building a life here.

The conversation starts with you

We’re here to listen.

There’s no preset list of issues you need to know about. Bring the questions, experiences, and concerns that matter to you. The purpose of Next North Van is to hear directly from the next generation.
