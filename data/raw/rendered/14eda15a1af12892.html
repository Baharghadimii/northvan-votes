About Betty

Your Voice for Common Sense

Experience

Betty is very grateful and honoured for the trust her community has given her for the past 8 years to strive to do the right thing for the whole community.

As a fourth-generation North Vancouverite who is proud of her heritage, Betty has deep roots and a passionate understanding of all that the community has to offer. She raised her two sons in Lynn Valley and is afraid she will be the last generation of her family that can afford to live here.

As an accountant with over 30 years of experience in private, public, small business, corporate, government, and investment markets, Betty applies her years of experience judiciously — analyzing financial information, applying decision criteria, actively listening, and taking a collaborative approach to deliver effective solutions. Her experience includes 5 years in a senior finance position in the Finance Department of the District of North Vancouver.

Betty believes one of the most important roles for a council member is to listen to their community's voices. The community is usually on the front line of what they are speaking about and deserves to be fully heard.
