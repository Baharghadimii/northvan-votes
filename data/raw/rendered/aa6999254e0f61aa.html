Platform & Issues

The District of North Vancouver is home to a growing community with divergent needs and significant issues. Betty is committed to ensuring community voices are heard at the decision table.

North Shore Wastewater Treatment Plant

Metro Vancouver is responsible for the wastewater treatment plant on District lands at 107 Pemberton Ave. This project has been a major concern for Betty and the community.

Original Budget

$700M

2022 Budget

$1.05B

Latest Projection

$3.86B

On September 27, 2021, Metro Vancouver's CAO attended a DNV Council meeting to report the contractor was "having trouble staying on time and on budget." Just 36 hours later, the General Contractor abandoned the project. The budget has since ballooned to $3.86 billion with no explanation for the $2.81 billion increase. Metro Vancouver has not explained what specifically caused the increase.

Provincial Housing Legislation

Since 2023, the provincial government has passed mandated Bills that fundamentally change how local governments plan, consider, and approve development. Council has lost some of their historical decision roles, and citizens have lost opportunities to speak and affect decisions.

Bill 44 — Housing Statutes (Residential Development) Amendment Act, 2023

  • Mandates increased density (small-scale multi-unit housing) in certain single-family and duplex zones.
  • Local governments can no longer hold Public Hearings for residential developments meeting the new rules — taking away the community's voice.
  • OCP and Zoning Bylaw must be amended by Dec. 31, 2025 to permit 20 years of residential growth; OCP must be updated every 5 years.

Bill 46 — Housing Statutes (Development Financing) Amendment Act, 2023

  • Changes to Development Cost Charge powers — can now charge developers for Police and Fire facilities.
  • Introduces Amenity Cost Charges (ACC) for public art, daycares, and enhanced public areas.
  • ACC charges may be waived for affordable housing projects.

Bill 47 — Housing Statutes (Transit-Oriented Areas) Amendment Act, 2023

  • New rules for development near major transit hubs (Phibbs Bus Exchange and Capilano University).
  • Minimum 12 storeys within 200m of a major transit hub; 8 storeys between 200–400m.
  • Local governments can no longer require off-street parking for residential use in these areas.

Bill 16 — Housing Statutes Amendment Act, 2024

  • New Inclusionary Zoning tool requiring a portion of new multi-family housing to be secured as affordable.
  • Updates the Density Bonus tool to secure affordable housing and amenities.
  • New authority to require developers to provide consistent support for renters.

Bill 25 — Housing and Municipal Affairs Statutes Amendment, 2025

  • Removes barriers to small-scale multi-unit housing, requiring 4–6 units per lot in traditional single-family areas.
  • At the April 13, 2026 Council meeting, Council passed a motion not to support SSMUH implementation as implied by Bill 25.
  • Betty voted with the majority — she doesn't want zoning decisions made in Victoria, or her community to lose their voices.
