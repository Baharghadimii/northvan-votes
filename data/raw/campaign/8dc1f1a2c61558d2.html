Getting Things Done

City Hall service standards

Make routine approvals predictable, measurable and fast.

Applicants should know the requirements, who is responsible for the file and when they can reasonably expect an answer. These standards cover the City’s portion of the process and do not waive provincial or other legal requirements.

5 business days

Straightforward small-project permits

For an application that is complete, compliant and within the City’s portion of the process.

10 business days

Straightforward single-family permits

For an application that is complete, compliant and within the City’s portion of the process.

1 week

Straightforward business licences

Where the use is permitted and all City requirements have been satisfied.

  • Publish a complete checklist before an applicant spends money.
  • Confirm zoning and basic eligibility early.
  • Assign one responsible point of contact.
  • Run internal reviews in parallel where possible rather than serially.
  • Track City-controlled delay, not merely total elapsed time.
  • Publish the percentage of files meeting the service standard.
  • Ask applicants for structured feedback and fix recurring causes of rework.
City Hall should be accountable for results, not just intentions.
