District of North Vancouver

A District for all to thrive for generations to come.

Why this election matters

Tough decisions about affordability and quality of life

The next Council will make tough decisions to balance affordability and quality of life. District residents are paying hundreds of millions of dollars for the over-runs at Metro Vancouver's Wastewater Treatment Plant. We cannot allow failures on that scale to happen again. We must invest responsibly in infrastructure, transportation, public facilities and services to maintain and improve the quality of life we've all come to enjoy.

Three decision priorities

What Council needs to get right

Accountability for major costs

Major capital projects need careful oversight and clear accountability. Council must understand costs and risks, test assumptions, demand clear reporting and explain the consequences to residents.

Responsible investment in daily life

Infrastructure, transportation, public facilities and services shape daily life in North Vancouver. The District must maintain what people rely on, plan ahead and invest with a clear understanding of long-term value and cost.

A community where people can make a good life

Affordability and quality of life belong in the same conversation. People at every stage of life should be able to build roots, stay connected and see a future in North Vancouver.

What Mathew brings

Diverse experience makes Council stronger

Every candidate cares deeply about our community. Council is strongest when its members each bring their unique experience to the table and test decisions from more than one perspective.

I bring two terms on District Council, a record of community volunteering and nonprofit leadership, and more than 15 years as a professional engineer working on major transportation infrastructure. I also know from my own life what it means to choose North Vancouver, work to find a suitable home, raise children here and manage the everyday cost of living in this community. Together, these experiences help me bring people together, work through complex problems and keep Council decisions grounded in how they affect daily life.

Why I’m running

A community where we can all make a good life

Our neighbours, friends and families can build roots, stay connected and thrive in North Vancouver at every stage of life. That vision guided me when I was elected to Council in 2014, a few months before my eldest daughter was born. She is now eleven and her sister is nine. Building a healthier, happier and more resilient community has never mattered more to me.

I am running again to help Council make responsible decisions, protect what people value about North Vancouver, and keep building a community where we can all make a good life.
