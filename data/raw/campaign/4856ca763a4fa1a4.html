Ask Mike
Why I’m running

I won’t be hard to find.

I love this city. North Vancouver has been my home for decades, and I built a software company here that created more than 20 highly skilled, well-paying local jobs. After a long career in business, I’m stepping away from it to serve this community again. A lot of people have told me their voice doesn’t matter at City Hall, and I think they have a point. I want a council that debates the hard questions instead of rubber-stamping them, with real public input before decisions are made, not after. Rebuilding that starts with showing up and listening, so here’s my promise: Show up. Listen. Lead.
