About Me

I have been a proud member of the District of North Vancouver Council since 2018. As a lifelong resident of the District, I have a deep passion for this community and am grateful to call Lynn Valley home with my wife, Signy, our two young children, Henry and Morgan, and our dog, Lola.

Serving on Council has given me the opportunity to engage with my community in new and meaningful ways. I have represented the DNV on the North Vancouver Chamber of Commerce, the DNV Library Board, the Museum and Archives Commission, and the Metro Vancouver Industrial Lands Strategy Task Force.
​
Beyond my work in public service, I have built a career in advertising sales over the past 20 years. After starting  at the North Shore News, I spent 11 years at Corus Entertainment, working in radio and television advertising with CKNW and Global Television. Today, I specialize in out-of-home advertising as a National Account Executive with PATTISON Outdoor Advertising.

Music has always been a significant part of my life. I earned a Bachelor of Music in 2007 from UBC, and while my career has taken a different path, I have remained active in the arts. For over 14 years, I have been a proud member of the internationally renowned Chor Leoni Men’s Choir.

In addition to my passion for the arts, I am a strong advocate for active, healthy living. An avid cyclist and triathlete, I have completed seven IRONMAN triathlons and more than a dozen marathons.
I am deeply committed to making the District of North Vancouver an even better place to live, work, and play.
I am grateful to live, work, raise my family, and serve our community on the unceded traditional territories of the Sḵwx̱wú7mesh (Squamish) and səlilwətaɬ (Tsleil-Waututh) Nations.
