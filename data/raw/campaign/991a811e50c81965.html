2026 Key Priorities

Posted byLinda MunroSeptember 1, 2026September 16, 2026Posted inUncategorized

My Priorities

  • HOUSING: Explore alternative housing options to better meet the needs of local people
  • ECONOMY: Prioritize maintaining and growing industry and commercial space so local residents can live and work in their community
  • TRANSPORTATION: Urge changes to combat traffic gridlock and encourage active transportation
  • COMMUNITY: Support additional childcare facilities so more families can find care close to home
  • CLIMATE: Add wildfire mitigation measures to DNV climate emergency action items 
  • INFRASTRUCTURE: Advocate for accountability from Metro Vancouver on the North Shore Waste Water Treatment Facility
