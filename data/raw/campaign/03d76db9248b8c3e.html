A Message From Don

To the Residents of the City of North Vancouver:

It has been a privilege to serve my community as a member of City Council.

I approach each issue before Council by listening carefully to the public, applying independent thinking, common sense, and using my experience in local government and business to seek the best decisions for our community.

There are many issues that I believe must continue to be priorities for the next Council term: increasing traffic congestion; rat-running vehicles through side streets; better parking solutions and regulations; future plans for Central Lonsdale; safer streets, paths, and sidewalks; mitigating conflicts between cars, pedestrians, cyclists and electric scooters. In addition, the need for truly affordable and workforce housing to help keep people on the North Shore; climate change action; increased child care; protecting mature trees; reducing tax increases for both residents and businesses; and more effective public engagement and transparency on major issues.

I support a truly independent public inquiry about Waste Water Treatment Plant costs, reducing future financial impact on our taxpayers and a review of Metro Vancouver Regional District structure.

I ask for your vote to continue serving you. Please contact me by phone or email to discuss any issues.

About Don

Dedicated to Serving North Vancouver

Don Bell has been a resident of North Vancouver for over 50 years, and has lived for the last twelve years in the Central Lonsdale area. He has extensive business, community and local political experience. Prior to his political career, Don owned and operated a small retail business in North Vancouver and for 13 years was in corporate management at Canada Safeway.

Political Experience and Community Involvement

  • Previously served as Member of Parliament for North Vancouver
  • Former District Mayor and Councillor for North Vancouver
  • Chair and Trustee of the North Vancouver School Board
  • Serves on the North Vancouver Museum & Archives Commission
  • Serves on the Park & Tilford Gardens Management Committee
  • Serves on the North Shore Advisory Committee on Disabilities Issues

Mail Ballot Voting

Vote by mail

All eligible electors can apply to vote by mail ballot.

  1. 1

    Apply online

    Complete the Mail Ballot Voting Application Form from August 31 to October 1, 2026, at 11:59 p.m.

    Apply online
  2. 2

    Receive your package

    Mail ballot packages with voting instructions will be mailed to eligible electors in late September/early October.

  3. 3

    Return your ballot

    Complete the ballot and required documents and return the sealed mail ballot package (via mail, in person, or by courier) to the Chief Election Officer before 8:00 p.m. on October 17, 2026.

It is the responsibility of the elector to ensure that their ballot is returned before the deadline.
