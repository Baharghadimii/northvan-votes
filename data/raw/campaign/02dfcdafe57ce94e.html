About

Raised here. Building our future here.

A fresh voice for North Vancouver. Grounded ideas, straight talk, and a campaign powered by neighbours like you.

I grew up in the District of North Vancouver and I'm proud that I still call it home. I went to school here, met my wife here, and our daughter was born at Lions Gate Hospital. Today we share our home with her grandparents, and there is nowhere else I would want to raise our family.

On weekends you'll find us walking the village, grabbing coffee at Delanys, or heading out on a trail run into the mountains. North Vancouver isn't just where I live. It's part of who I am.

My career has taken me from film sets, where solving problems quickly and practically is the whole job, into industry relations and policy work. That path has given me a practical, grounded approach to complex problems and a deep appreciation for how decisions made at the table ripple out into people's everyday lives.

I am running for Council because I want DNV to be for my daughter what it has been for our family: a genuine community, built for the long term, where people put down roots and stay.
