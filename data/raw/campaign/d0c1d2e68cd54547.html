Tony’s Actions on Council

As a City Councillor, Tony has brought advanced practical, community-focused ideas at Council. These actions addressed health, accessibility, public safety, environmental protection, democratic participation, and active transportation.

They include:

  • Supporting living kidney donors through the Kidney Foundation’s Living Donor Circle of Excellence
  • Encouraging safer use of e-bikes, e-scooters, and other electric mobility devices
  • Supporting first responder mental health and resiliency programs
  • Calling for overdue provincial funding for public libraries
  • Updating election rules to better support citizen engagement
  • Creating safer spaces for skateboarders
  • Addressing retractable balcony glass systems in residential buildings
  • Calling for provincial COVID-19 support for homeowners
  • Advancing protected mobility lanes for people of all ages and abilities
  • Ending the use of anticoagulant rodenticides on City-owned property
  • Supporting bike valet services at City events

Together, these motions reflect Tony’s commitment to safer streets, healthier communities, better public services, stronger local democracy, and a more accessible and sustainable City of North Vancouver.
