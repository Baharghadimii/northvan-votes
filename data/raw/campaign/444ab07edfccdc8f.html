Your neighbourhood

The 2026 election, one community area at a time

The District of North Vancouver describes itself as eight community areas. Each page below says where to vote, which all-candidates meeting is nearby, what the District lists for the area, and how Max Zahedi's priorities apply there.

Lynn Valley

One of the District's longest-established communities and one of its designated town centres.

Upper Lynn · Upper West Lynn · Lower West Lynn · Valley Centre · Central Lynn · Lynn Canyon · West Lynn Terrace · Boundary · Kirkstone

Voting, meetings and Max's proposals for Lynn Valley

Lynn Creek

The District calls Lynn Creek one of its new town centres: bounded by Lynn Valley to the north, the waterfront industries to the south, the City of North Vancouver to the west and Highway 1 to the east, with new homes within walking distance of Phibbs Exchange, and home to Capilano University and to Seylynn, Bridgman and Inter River parks.

Lower Lynn · Lynnmour

Voting, meetings and Max's proposals for Lynn Creek

Maplewood

One of the four town centres designated in the Official Community Plan, close to the waterfront and to business and industry, with the Seymour River, the Burrard Inlet foreshore and mudflats, Maplewood Farm, Cutter Island Park and the Windridge Escarpment.

Voting, meetings and Max's proposals for Maplewood

Seymour

The largest community area in the District, bounded by the Seymour River, Deep Cove, Burrard Inlet and the forests of Mount Seymour.

Riverside · Seymour Heights · Blueridge · McCartney Woods · Windridge · Windsor Park · Northlands · Parkway · Parkgate · Indian River · Roche Point

Voting, meetings and Max's proposals for Seymour

Deep Cove

A small, waterfront-oriented village centre at the eastern edge of the District, bounded by Seymour, Burrard Inlet, Indian Arm and the mountains.

Deep Cove village · Cove Cliff · Dollarton · Indian Arm

Voting, meetings and Max's proposals for Deep Cove

Upper Capilano

The northwest corner of the District, on the slopes below Grouse Mountain, bounded by Highway 1 to the south and Delbrook / Upper Lonsdale to the east, with its commercial and community heart in Edgemont Village, which the District is refreshing.

Grousewoods · Cleveland · Canyon Heights · Handsworth · Highlands · Edgemont · Edgemont Village

Voting, meetings and Max's proposals for Upper Capilano

Delbrook / Upper Lonsdale

A primarily residential area between Upper Capilano, Lynn Valley and the City of North Vancouver, known for its views and park space.

Delbrook · Upper Delbrook · Norwood Queens · Carisbrooke · Braemar · Upper Lonsdale

Voting, meetings and Max's proposals for Delbrook

Lions Gate / Lower Capilano

The western gateway to the District and the first community after the Lions Gate Bridge, with the commercial area along Marine Drive.

Lower Capilano · Woodcroft · Pemberton Heights · Pemberton · Norgate

Voting, meetings and Max's proposals for Lions Gate

Confirmed fact The eight areas and the neighbourhoods inside them are the District of North Vancouver's own, from its "In your neighbourhood" pages, read September 12, 2026. Central Lonsdale, Lower Lonsdale, Grand Boulevard and Calverhall are in the City of North Vancouver, a separate municipality that elects its own mayor and council on the same day. If you live there, this election is not yours — the City's is.
