CAMPAIGN PRIORITIES

Four connected priorities

These priorities reflect Manoochehr’s commitment to students, schools, and the community and will continue to be strengthened through listening, research, and dialogue.

♙

Student Success

Every student deserves meaningful support to learn, grow, and reach their potential.

  • Listen to students, families, educators, and staff about barriers to learning.
  • Support thoughtful decisions that recognize different learning needs.
  • Keep student well-being and long-term success central to Board discussions.
✓

Safe & Inclusive Schools

Schools should be welcoming, respectful environments where every student can feel safe and included.

  • Support respectful school cultures and a strong sense of belonging.
  • Value constructive dialogue among students, families, and educators.
  • Approach safety and inclusion with care, evidence, and community listening.
◇

Community Partnership

Strong schools benefit from clear communication and genuine collaboration.

  • Encourage accessible, two-way engagement with parents and guardians.
  • Respect the knowledge and experience of educators, staff, and community partners.
  • Strengthen connections between schools and the communities they serve.
⌂

Smart Planning & Strong Resources

Responsible planning can help public resources support students effectively today and over the long term.

  • Ask clear questions about priorities, budgets, facilities, and future needs.
  • Bring project-management and planning experience to Board deliberations.
  • Support transparent decision-making and responsible stewardship.
