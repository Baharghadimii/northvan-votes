01 The platform

Seven commitments for a City That Works.

The campaign now has a complete seven-part platform. Each area begins with a clear commitment and keeps the supporting detail available for people who want to look deeper.

Read the complete platform
01

Getting Things Done

Clear service standards, productive administration, responsible budgets and public accountability for major projects.

02

Local Economy

Faster approvals, stronger commercial streets and practical support for the businesses already investing here.

03

Housing That Fits People’s Lives

Homes with useful layouts, secure tenure and room for people to stay through different stages of life.

04

Getting Around

Transportation ideas that fit North Vancouver and make local and regional trips more reliable.

05

Growth & Neighbourhood Planning

Meaningful choices before major decisions, protected public assets and infrastructure alongside growth.

06

Parks, Recreation & Public Life

Useful shared spaces, disciplined recreation projects and public places designed for everyday community life.

07

Safety, Accessibility & Community

The right response from the right service, with accessibility and community needs considered from the beginning.

02 The standard

What a City That Works should mean.

Local government should make everyday life easier to navigate—and be candid about how well it is performing.

01

Clear standards

Tell people what to expect from City Hall, measure performance and publish the results.

02

Better daily life

Plan housing, transportation, parks and services around how people actually live.

03

Accountable decisions

Explain the tradeoffs, show what changed and take responsibility for the outcome.

03 About Dave

Practical leadership, grounded in experience.

Dave Kurvers has spent more than three decades working in British Columbia’s film and television industry, where planning, coordination and solving complex problems are part of the job.

His background also includes public-policy leadership, service on Elections BC’s Election Advisory Committee, and university study in human and economic geography and business administration.

Dave is running for Mayor to make City Hall more responsive, accountable and focused on results for North Vancouver residents and businesses.
