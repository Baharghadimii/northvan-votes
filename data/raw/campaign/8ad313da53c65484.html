About Tammy Bentz

I want to add a bold new voice to Council.

I’m running for City Council because I love North Vancouver. I love the ocean, trails, and mountains that make it beautiful but I love this vibe of living in a connected community that it offers even more.

I want to serve on a city council that’s responsive, accountable, and bold. I want this community to feel valued and looked after. I am at a stage in life where I have time to dedicate my time to work as one of our community’s stewards and ensure our city remains a leader in building a sustainable, inclusive, and financially responsible future. We can protect the natural environment that defines our city while creating complete neighbourhoods where people of all ages can afford to live, work, and thrive.

I want to listen to neighbours and bring their ideas forward to council. I want a community where we are all heard.

My Priorities

My background

  • Already a passionate advocate for my community in many areas, I want to work together with neighbours to benefit each and all of us. I’m optimistic with practical perseverance & dogged tenacity.
  • Grateful CNV’er who’s raised my kid here, volunteering on little league teams, school committees, & community climate justice groups
  • Communication has been my livelihood as Faculty of BCIT (School of Business & Media) and working as a Script Supervisor, Director, Writer, Producer in the Film Industry.
  • Former small business co-owner
  • 2SLGBTQ+ volunteering with the North Shore Pride Alliance
  • Past member on various Boards of Directors, for Arts organizations & non-profit societies
  • Big fan of beach walks, trail hikes, watching skateboarders/ snowboarders/ mountainbikers, public discourse, libraries & reading, cats, and good food

My principles

If elected, every decision I make at City Hall will be guided by five principles:

  • Govern openly, collaboratively, and with integrity
  • Invest wisely and respect taxpayers while promoting livable wages
  • Make it easier to walk, cycle, take transit, or drive safely
  • Protect our environment while protecting residents from the impacts of climate change
  • Build more homes/make homes greener while maintaining neighbourhood livability

North Vancouver can continue to grow into a more affordable, sustainable, welcoming, and resilient city. By working together with residents, businesses, Indigenous partners, and other governments, we can build a community that offers opportunity, protects what makes our city special, and delivers practical results for today’s residents and for future generations.
