Commitments

My commitments, in writing.

Campaign promises are easy to make and easy to forget. This page exists so you can hold me to mine.

Below is every commitment I have made, written plainly enough that you can tell whether I kept it. Each one has a status you can check. If a commitment changes, gets delayed or turns out to be the wrong idea once I see the full picture from the inside, I will say so here and explain why. I would rather tell you I changed my mind than quietly hope you stop reading.

Nothing on this page disappears. Every edit is recorded, with the date and the reason.
