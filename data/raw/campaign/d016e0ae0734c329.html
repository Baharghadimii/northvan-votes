Mountain Highway and East Keith Road

Plan for traffic before adding 1,000 parking spots.

Mountain Highway and East Keith Road are already a critical pressure point for neighbourhood traffic. A proposal that adds 1,000 parking spaces must come with a public transportation plan that accounts for every new vehicle movement, protects local streets, and keeps the corridor moving.

Graham will press for a transparent traffic-impact review, coordinated signals and intersections, safe walking and cycling connections, and clear mitigation commitments before this scale of parking is approved. Growth must solve congestion, not lock it in.
