About Manoochehr

Experience guided by service

A professional problem-solver, mentor, immigrant, parent, and North Vancouver resident who believes education shapes stronger communities.

“Building strong schools means building stronger futures.”

A professional and community-minded perspective

Manoochehr Azizi is a City of North Vancouver resident, architect, mentor, and community volunteer. His work and community service have centred on listening carefully, bringing people together, understanding complex needs, and making responsible decisions.

Connection to North Vancouver

Manoochehr lives in the City of North Vancouver and is preparing to serve the community where he and his family have built their lives. He values the city’s strong sense of place, its diversity, and the important role public schools play in creating opportunity and belonging.

Why he is running

Manoochehr is running because he believes careful listening, responsible planning, and respectful collaboration can help the Board of Education support students, educators, families, and the wider community. His mother’s work as a teacher helped shape his respect for education and public service.
