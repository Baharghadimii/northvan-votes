Beyond the article

Graham's background,
qualifications, and record.

The candidate article is one part of Graham Alexander's story. His experience spans electrical engineering, complex-project delivery, real estate development, global business operations, and a World Bank Group-administered international arbitration.

Engineering and delivery

Technical judgment backed by practical experience.

Graham is a UBC Electrical Engineering graduate. His work in development and business has required him to manage complex systems, coordinate people and partners, evaluate risk, control costs, and carry projects from planning through delivery.

Global business leadership

Building and operating beyond a single market.

As founder of StayRunners, Graham has operated a worldwide technology-enabled business. He brings an operator's perspective on accountability, execution, team coordination, and making resources work harder.

Mount Thurston and Mount Archibald

Development experience in Chilliwack.

Through GGIQ Developments, Graham has worked on projects including Lynn Creek, Chilliwack Estates, Mount Thurston Drive, and Mount Archibald Place. The Mount Thurston and Mount Archibald work reflects the long-term planning, infrastructure coordination, and persistence needed to bring a challenging hillside community forward.

International arbitration

Standing up in a World Bank Group forum.

Graham was a Canadian claimant in Carlos Sastre and others v. United Mexican States (ICSID Case No. UNCT/20/2), an arbitration administered by the World Bank Group's International Centre for Settlement of Investment Disputes. The tribunal issued its award in 2022.

A compassionate DNV

A real path forward on homelessness.

Graham's plan centres dignity, recovery, and safer public spaces for everyone.

The proposal calls for phased recovery supports—safe shelter, food, mental-health and rehabilitation services, transitional housing, and pathways to work and independent living—subject to feasibility, consultation, and required approvals.
