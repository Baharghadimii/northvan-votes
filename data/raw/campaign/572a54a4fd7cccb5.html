About Max

Born on the North Shore, I want to give back to our community! I’m a 32 year old father, education assistant, SFU alumni with a major in political science, with passions in data and research analysis, good food and board games.

Learn More

Why I'm Running

I’m running because I believe that our city can always be better. That it is time to elect those that not only understand our current communities’ issues but are able to look beyond and envision what our future could be.

Learn More

Key Issues

There are many important issues that both positively and negatively affect our community. These issues include; Housing, Transportation, Recreational Services and Cost of Living.
