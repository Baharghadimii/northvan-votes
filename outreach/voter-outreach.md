# Getting northvanvotes.ca in front of voters

Advance voting opens **Wed Oct 7**. Election day **Sat Oct 17**.
The only hard rule: **never pay to promote it.** Organic posting is unrestricted;
a boosted post would make it election advertising under LECFA.

---

## 1. North Shore News — do this first

One email, the largest single audience, and exactly the right people. They cover
these races closely and "local resident builds free non-partisan guide" is a real
story in election week. Even a two-line mention in a roundup beats everything else
on this list.

**To:** editor@nsnews.com (also try their election reporter's byline address)
**Subject:** North Van resident built a free, non-partisan guide to all 59 candidates

> Hi,
>
> I'm a North Vancouver resident and I've built northvanvotes.ca — a free guide
> to the October 17 election covering all 59 candidates across the City, the
> District and SD44.
>
> It's organised by issue rather than by candidate: you pick housing, or traffic,
> or schools, and read what every candidate has actually said about it, quoted
> word for word with a link to the source. There's also a search over everything
> they've said, pages on specific local arguments like the wastewater plant cost
> overruns and Harry Jerome, and a side-by-side comparison tool.
>
> No ratings, no endorsements, no advertising, no tracking. Everything comes from
> candidates' own filed statements and campaign websites. The method and its
> limits are documented at northvanvotes.ca/methodology.
>
> I'm not affiliated with any candidate or campaign. Happy to answer anything.
>
> Bahareh Ghadimi
> hello@northvanvotes.ca

## 2. Community Facebook groups — where people actually are

Search Facebook for these and post as yourself. Read each group's rules first;
most allow non-commercial community info but ban anything that smells like
promotion, so lead with what it does and be open that you made it.

- North Vancouver Community / North Vancouver BC Community
- Lynn Valley / Lynn Valley Community
- Deep Cove & Seymour
- Edgemont Village
- Lower Lonsdale / Central Lonsdale
- North Shore Mums / North Shore Parents (strong for the trustee races)
- Buy Nothing groups — surprisingly active and very local

**Post:**

> 59 people are running for mayor, council and school trustee in North
> Vancouver, and advance voting opens Wednesday.
>
> I couldn't find a straight answer on where any of them stood without reading
> 59 websites, so I spent a few weeks building northvanvotes.ca.
>
> Pick an issue — traffic, housing, schools, taxes — and read what every
> candidate actually said about it. Their words, quoted exactly, with a link to
> where they said it. There's a search box if you only care about one thing.
>
> Free, no ads, no tracking, no ratings or endorsements. I'm not connected to
> any candidate or campaign. If I've got something wrong, tell me and I'll fix it.

**Shorter, for groups that dislike long posts:**

> Advance voting starts Wednesday and there are 59 candidates. I built
> northvanvotes.ca so you can pick an issue and read what each of them actually
> said about it, in their own words. Free, non-partisan, no ads.

## 5. The two libraries

North Vancouver City Library and North Vancouver District Public Library both
curate election resources and are trusted locally. Same email as above, addressed
to their information or community services desk.

---

## 6. r/northvancouver

Small but precisely the audience, and Reddit rewards being straightforward.
Title: *"I built a free guide to all 59 North Van candidates, sorted by issue"*.
Use the long post above and say plainly that you made it.

## 7. The three remaining all-candidates meetings

The highest-intent voters in the city will be in these rooms.

- **Mon Oct 6, 7pm** — Lynn Valley Library Community Room (Better North Shore)
- **Wed Oct 8, 7pm** — Mount Seymour United Church (Seymour/Blueridge/Deep Cove)
- **Tue Oct 13, 4:45pm** — Seycove Secondary

Email the organisers beforehand. A mention from the chair reaches the whole room.
Printed cards with a QR code left on the seats cost almost nothing.
