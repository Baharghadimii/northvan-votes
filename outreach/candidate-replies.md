## Linda Munro — four priorities missing

**Subject:** Re: your North Van Votes profile

Hi Linda,

Thank you for writing, and sorry — you were right about all of it. Two separate
bugs on my end, and between them they took out most of your page.

The first one: my crawler compares web addresses literally, and your site links
to itself as lindamunro.ca while the address I had was www.lindamunro.ca. It
treated those as two different websites, threw away every internal link, and
stopped at your front page. So I never opened your priorities page at all.

The second: I was dropping any quote under fifteen words as a fragment. Your
priorities are one line each and four of them come in under that. "CLIMATE: Add
wildfire mitigation measures to DNV climate emergency action items" is eleven
words. It got binned as noise.

Both are fixed. Your page now carries all five priorities, each filed under its
own topic, and you appear on the transportation, local economy, childcare and
climate pages:

https://northvanvotes.ca/candidates/dnv-council-munro-linda/

You were a long way from the only one this was happening to. Fixing it added
117 passages across 36 of the 59 candidates. I've written up both bugs on the
methodology page, since people should be able to see what the site got wrong.

One thing I can't do, and I want to be straight about why: the North Shore News
answer. I only use candidates' own filed statements and their campaign
websites. I don't take anything from news coverage, all-candidates meetings,
interviews or social media, for anybody. Making an exception here would be
unfair to the other 58 people on the site. If you put that material on
lindamunro.ca I'll pick it up on the next run.

Bahareh
northvanvotes.ca
